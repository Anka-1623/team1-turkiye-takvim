# Team1 brand files used by this site

Unmodified copies from the Team1 design system (`avalancheteam1/design-system`, skill `team1-design-system` v2.2.0,
authority snapshot 2026-08-12). Do not redraw, recolor, crop or re-export them; replace a file only with a newer
approved original and update this list.

| File here | Source path in the design system | Status | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Team1_Symbol_Main.svg` | `assets/identity/symbol/Team1_Symbol_Main.svg` | current-global-core | Reserved: compact placements |
| `team1turkiye.svg` | `assets/chapters/turkiye/team1turkiye.svg` | current-optional-verify-owner-approval | The only logo on the site: header and footer (on a black plate in the light theme), Open Graph card |

The site shows the Türkiye lockup alone, with no global Team1 wordmark and no Avalanche mark. The lockup only exists
with white lettering, so the light theme places it on a black plate rather than recoloring it; a dark-lettered
original from the chapter owner would replace that plate.

Favicons come from `assets/identity/favicon/`: `favicon.ico`, `icon.png` and `apple-icon.png` live in `src/app/`,
`icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` and `icon-maskable.png` in `public/` (referenced by `src/app/manifest.ts`).

`src/app/team1-tokens.css` is the design system's `tokens/design-tokens.css`, unchanged.
